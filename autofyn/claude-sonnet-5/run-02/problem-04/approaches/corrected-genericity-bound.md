### What was wrong (round 2) and the fix (round 4)

Round 2's §2/§3 derived which "clean" (junk-free, i.e. independent of Shan-Yu's
transcendental starting angles $p_0,q_0$) values are reachable using a closure operator
generated by (halve: $a\mapsto a/2$) and (reflect: $a\mapsto V-a$ relative to a moving
doubling target $V_i=2^i\theta$). This silently omits the **shift** move
($a\mapsto a-\theta$, certified in `lemmas/transfer-and-shift-moves.md`) and gives the
too-small family $\{180/((2^k+1)2^j)\}$ — refuted last round by the proof-reviewer's
independent hand-verification of the exact-fraction 8-move witness for θ=180°/7°
(genuinely forceable, but $7\ne(2^k+1)2^j$ for any $k,j\ge0$).

The fix, independently converged on by two round-4 explorers (with a 20,000-trial random
sweep over shift/halve sequences finding zero counterexamples, and a hand-derived closed
form): the correct closure operator is generated by (halve) and (**shift-by-θ**) acting
on the seed $180°-\theta$ (the fixed output of the transfer move) — NOT (reflect relative
to a moving target). This is a strictly simpler object than round 2's, and it is the
object that actually matches the real sufficiency construction in
`lemmas/theta-180-over-n-forceable.md`.

### 1. Shan-Yu's adversarial construction (unchanged from round 2, re-stated)

Fix target θ∈(0°,90°]. Choose $p_0,q_0>0$ algebraically independent transcendentals
over $\mathbb Q(\theta)$ (a transcendence basis of $\mathbb R/\mathbb Q(\theta)$, rescaled
by positive rationals to make $p_0,q_0>0$, $p_0+q_0<180$ — rescaling by rationals
preserves algebraic independence). Set $r_0=180-p_0-q_0>0$. Shan-Yu starts with
$(p_0,q_0,r_0)$.

### 2. Junk-coefficient representation (unchanged from round 2)

Every angle ever present, starting from $(p_0,q_0,r_0)$, is representable (using
$r_0=180-p_0-q_0$ to eliminate $r_0$) as
$$a = A + B\theta + Cp_0 + Dq_0,\qquad A,B,C,D\in\mathbb Q,$$
provided every cut parameter $x_1$ ever chosen is itself a $\mathbb Q$-affine
combination of currently-present angles and θ (the "wild $x_1$" case is handled
separately in §5). Call $(C,D)$ the **junk coefficients**; $a$ is **clean** iff
$(C,D)=(0,0)$.

### 3. Propagation lemma (re-verified this round, with shift now explicitly folded in)

For every move of the four certified types, junk coefficients propagate as an explicit
$\mathbb Q$-linear combination of the operands':

- **(halve)** $a\mapsto a/2$: $(C,D)\mapsto(C/2,D/2)$. Injective on
  $\mathbb Q^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}\to$ itself: halving a nonzero rational pair stays
  nonzero.
- **(shift-by-θ)** $a\mapsto a\mp\theta$: since θ has junk $(0,0)$ (it is the fixed target,
  not derived from $p_0,q_0$), $a\mp\theta$ has **exactly the same** $(C,D)$ as $a$. This
  is the one entry missing from round 2's propagation table — it is now made explicit and
  is immediate from linearity of the representation.
- **(transfer)**, spectator $r<\theta$, partner $p>\theta$: the surviving branch is
  $\{r,\theta-r,180-\theta\}$. The entry $180-\theta$ is **literally clean**, $(0,0)$,
  regardless of the junk coefficients of whatever was "big" before — it is a fresh
  injection, not derived from propagating $p$'s junk. The entries $r$ and $\theta-r$ keep
  the spectator $r$'s own junk coefficients (θ is clean, so $\theta-r$ has junk $-(C_r,D_r)$
  where $(C_r,D_r)$ is $r$'s junk).
- **(bisection double-hit / altitude double-hit)**: these are instances of (halve) /
  direct substitution respectively; no new propagation rule needed.

**Consequence:** the only move that ever turns a junky lineage clean is transfer (a
genuine reset, injecting the always-clean constant $180-\theta$); halve and shift never
create or destroy clean status of the entry they act on.

### 4. Wild-$x_1$ exclusion (tightened this round — the round-2 gap flagged by explorers)

If some $x_1$ chosen by Mulan is **not** a $\mathbb Q$-affine combination of
currently-present angles and θ, two sub-cases:

- **$x_1$ transcendental over $K_i:=\mathbb Q(\theta,p_0,q_0)(\text{angles present so
  far})$.** Extend the basis to $\{1,\theta,p_0,q_0,x_1\}$ (algebraically independent
  over $\mathbb Q$ by construction). Any later angle carrying a nonzero coefficient on
  $x_1$ cannot equal a fixed target $2^i\theta\in K_i$ (that would be a nontrivial
  $\mathbb Q$-linear relation contradicting independence) — so such a branch is provably
  useless for hitting θ (or any $2^i\theta$ needed for D1).
- **$x_1$ algebraic over $K_i$ but $x_1\notin K_i$** (round 2's unproved "routine
  extension" case — **this round's open gap, not yet closed**): let $f(X)\in K_i[X]$ be
  $x_1$'s minimal polynomial over $K_i$, degree $d\ge2$ (degree 1 would mean $x_1\in K_i$).
  Every subsequent angle is a $K_i$-linear combination of $1,x_1,x_1^2,\dots,x_1^{d-1}$
  (since the cut formula only ever adds/subtracts/halves — never multiplies two present
  angles together — so no power of $x_1$ higher than 1 is ever produced by a SINGLE move,
  but $x_1$ itself, once introduced, persists across later moves as a formal quantity, so
  cross terms don't arise either; hence in fact every later angle is $K_i$-affine in
  $x_1$, degree ≤1, not degree $d-1$). An affine-in-$x_1$ quantity $u+vx_1$ ($u,v\in K_i$)
  equals a fixed target $\tau\in K_i$ (namely θ or $2^i\theta$) iff $v(x_1-\tau/v+u/v)=0$
  requires, since $x_1\notin K_i$ (so $x_1$ is not any $K_i$-constant), either $v=0$ (then
  $u=\tau$, a relation purely among $K_i$-elements, not involving $x_1$ at all — fine, no
  contradiction, this is the "doesn't matter" case) or $v\ne0$ forcing $x_1=(\tau-u)/v\in K_i$,
  contradicting $x_1\notin K_i$. So **any angle with $v\ne0$ (nonzero coefficient on the
  newly-introduced $x_1$) can never equal a fixed target** — the same conclusion as the
  transcendental case, now justified for the algebraic case too (this closes the gap
  round 2 waved at, using only that the cut formula is affine, never producing $x_1^2$ or
  higher within one formula application — **this sub-argument needs the builder to verify
  carefully that $x_1$ genuinely never recombines with itself across multiple moves in a
  way that could produce a higher-degree or non-affine dependence**; flagged as the
  step requiring the most care).

**Consequence:** WLOG every $x_1$ ever used lies in $K_i$ at the time it's chosen; any
move that doesn't satisfies this is provably useless and can be excluded from
consideration.

### 5. The corrected closure computation (the actual fix)

Define
$$C^*(\theta) := \text{closure of } \{180°-\theta\} \text{ under (halve) } a\mapsto a/2 \text{ and (shift) } a\mapsto a-\theta \text{ (valid while } a>\theta).$$

**Claim (closed form — proof sketch, not yet fully formalized):** every element of
$C^*(\theta)$ has the form
$$x = \frac{180-(k+1)\theta}{2^A},\qquad k,A\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.$$

*Proof idea.* By induction on the number of operations. Track a sequence of $A$ total
halvings, with $b_i\ge0$ shifts occurring between the $i$-th and $(i{+}1)$-th halving
($0\le i\le A$, $b_i=0$ allowed). Starting from $y_0=180-\theta$: after the shifts before
the first halving, the value is $180-\theta-b_0\theta=180-(b_0+1)\theta$; halving gives
$[180-(b_0+1)\theta]/2$; the shifts before the second halving subtract $b_1\theta$ from
this (each shift subtracts $\theta$ from the *current* value, not from a rescaled copy),
so after the second halving the value is $\big([180-(b_0+1)\theta]/2 - b_1\theta\big)/2 =
[180-(b_0+1)\theta - 2b_1\theta]/4$. Iterating, after $A$ halvings interleaved with
$b_0,\dots,b_A$ shifts, the value is
$$\frac{180 - (b_0+1)\theta - \sum_{i=1}^{A} b_i 2^i\theta}{2^A} = \frac{180-(k+1)\theta}{2^A},\qquad k:=b_0+\sum_{i=1}^A b_i 2^i\ \ge0\text{ (an integer)}.$$
Every nonnegative integer $k$ arises this way (take $A=0,b_0=k$ for the simplest witness,
or spread $k$'s binary digits across the $b_i$'s for others), so the closed form is
exactly $\{[180-(k+1)\theta]/2^A : k,A\ge0\}$. $\blacksquare$ *(This is the round-2 sketch
formalized; the builder should double check the "shift subtracts from the current halved
value, not a stale copy" bookkeeping carefully — it is correct but easy to mis-index.)*

**Setting $x=\theta$** (win condition for θ itself) or $x=2\theta$ (needed to feed the D1
bisection double-hit): both give $180=\theta\cdot n$ for a positive integer
$n=k+1+2^A\ge2$ (minimal case $k=0,A=0\Rightarrow n=2$), and **every** integer $n\ge2$
arises (e.g. $A=0,k=n-2$). So
$$C^*(\theta)\ni\theta\ (\text{or }2\theta)\iff \theta = 180°/n,\ n\ge2\text{ integer}.$$

### 6. Combining §§2–5: necessity

The junk-free angles ever guaranteed present (under WLOG-affine play, §4) are exactly
$C^*(\theta)$: transfer injects the clean seed $180-\theta$ (§3); halve and shift act on
clean angles exactly as in §5's closure (§3: they preserve clean status of the angle they
touch); nothing else creates or destroys clean status. By §4, no junky angle (nonzero
$(C,D)$) can ever equal θ or $2\theta$ for the *specific* algebraically independent
$p_0,q_0$ Shan-Yu chose (a nontrivial linear relation would contradict independence).
Hence:
$$\theta\in S \implies \theta\text{ (or }2\theta\text{, via D1) is clean and lies in }C^*(\theta) \implies \theta=180°/n,\ n\ge2.$$

Combined with `lemmas/non-obtuse-invariant.md` (θ≤90° necessary) and
`lemmas/theta-180-over-n-forceable.md` (sufficiency, imported):
$$\boxed{S = \{180°/n : n\in\mathbb Z,\ n\ge2\}}.$$
