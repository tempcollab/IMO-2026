## imo-2026-02

### Setup used
WLOG place BC on the real axis: B, C real, A = Ax + i·Ay (Ay>0). Then M=(A+B)/2,
N=(A+C)/2 automatically have Im(M)=Im(N)=Ay/2, so **MN is horizontal**, and the
perpendicular bisector of MN is the *vertical* line x = (Mx+Nx)/2 =: target,
target = (2·Ax+Bx+Cx)/4. Hence **OM=ON ⟺ Re(O) = target** (this reduction is exact
and elementary, no extra hypothesis needed — it's just "M,N have equal height").
K, L were parametrized exactly as the dispatch suggested: K on ray from B at
signed angle α to BA (K = B + tK·(A−B)/|A−B|·e^{−iα}), L on ray from C at signed
angle α to CA (L = C + tL·(A−C)/|A−C|·e^{iα}), with (α, tK, tL) the 3 free real
parameters (2 eqs left over → 1-parameter family, matching the synthetic
explorer's finding).

### Result 1 (upgrades prior round's numeric-only claim to an exact algebraic identity)
Using sympy on symbolic A,K,L (no B,C needed except via A'x=(Bx+Cx)/2), I computed
the **exact circumcenter x-coordinate formula** of triangle AKL and compared it
against `target`. Writing u = K−A, v = L−A (as vectors), cross(u,v)=ux·vy−uy·vx:

  Re(O) − target  =  [ (Ax−A'x)·cross(u,v) + (vy|u|² − uy|v|²) ] / D

where D = 2[(A'x−Ax)(uy) ... ] is exactly the usual nonzero circumcenter
denominator (2× signed area of AKL, ≠0 for non-degenerate AKL). Call the bracket
`myexpr`. So **OM=ON ⟺ myexpr = 0** exactly, for ANY K,L (not just the family
satisfying the 3 angle conditions) — this is a pure coordinate identity, no
geometry hypothesis used yet.

Separately I formed the concyclicity determinant of A,K,L,A* where
**A* = (A'x, Ay)** (exactly the point the synthetic explorer described: same
height as A, but shifted so its foot on BC is the midpoint A' of BC — the
"rectangle" construction). Sympy gives, exactly:

  det_concyclic(A,K,L,A*) = (A'x − Ax) · myexpr

**So for any triangle with A'x ≠ Ax (i.e. AB ≠ AC), "A,K,L,A* concyclic" and
"OM=ON" are algebraically EQUIVALENT** — this makes the prior round's numerically-
conjectured equivalence a proven exact identity (sympy-verified symbolically,
not just checked at sample points), modulo the elementary two-line coordinate
reduction above. See `/tmp/geo2.py`–`geo4.py` in this session for the sympy
derivations (not saved to repo; can be regenerated in ~5 lines each).

**Isosceles caveat (new, not previously flagged):** when AB=AC, A'x=Ax, so A*=A
and the concyclicity determinant is identically 0 no matter what myexpr is — the
concyclicity route *degenerates* in this one special case and does NOT by itself
establish myexpr=0. The outliner must either (a) treat AB=AC as a small separate
case (e.g. by a continuity/limiting argument from the non-isosceles identity,
since myexpr and Re(O)−target are polynomial hence continuous in the triangle's
shape, or (b) prove myexpr=0 directly there — myexpr itself never degenerates, only
the "divide by (A'x−Ax)" step does. This is a genuine but narrow gap to flag for
the outline, not a fundamental obstruction.

### Result 2: exact polynomial (non-transcendental) form of the two remaining
angle conditions
With K,L parametrized as above (so ∠KBA=∠ACL=α is already built into the ray
directions), the other two conditions ∠LBK=∠LNC and ∠LCK=∠BMK, correctly signed
(directed angles, verified numerically against a precise fsolve solution to
1e-14), are exactly:

  e1:  cross(L−B, K−B)·dot(L−N, C−N) − cross(L−N, C−N)·dot(L−B, K−B) = 0
  e2:  cross(L−C, K−C)·dot(B−M, K−M) − cross(B−M, K−M)·dot(L−C, K−C) = 0

(NOTE the correct pairing for e2 is dot(B−M,K−M), NOT dot(K−M,B−M) — the naive
guess with the latter fails; sign matters because these are *directed* angle
equalities, confirmed by checking signed argument values numerically before
trusting the polynomial form — see `/tmp/geo6.py`.) These are honest polynomials
in (tK, tL) of low degree once α is fixed (bilinear-type, since K linear in tK,
L linear in tL, cross/dot bilinear) — i.e. the whole problem, α included, can in
principle be posed as a purely algebraic system in (cos α, sin α, tK, tL) with
the unit-circle relation cos²α+sin²α=1, making a full symbolic
Gröbner-basis/resultant elimination (to certify myexpr=0 lies in the ideal
generated by e1, e2, and the unit-circle relation) a concrete fallback if the
synthetic angle-chase stalls — flagged as a heavy but available Plan B, not
attempted here (likely large but tractable for a CAS with the low degree
involved; a builder with sympy could try `sp.groebner` on this system).

### Distinct opening this lens contributes
A **direct coordinate/algebraic target for the outliner that does not require
first identifying A* synthetically**: prove the single scalar identity
`myexpr = (Ax−A'x)·cross(K−A,L−A) + Im(L−A)|K−A|² − Im(K−A)|L−A|² = 0` using the
three given angle conditions (e.g. by algebraically encoding all three as
directed-angle/cross-dot relations as in Result 2 and combining). This is
equivalent to, but computationally more concrete than, "A,K,L,A* concyclic" and
sidesteps the isosceles-degeneracy of the A*-concyclicity route entirely (myexpr
itself never degenerates). It is also equivalent to "O lies on the perpendicular
bisector of MN", i.e. directly the problem's own target, giving a route that
never needs to introduce A* if the outliner prefers to work with raw coordinates
plus the polynomial angle conditions from Result 2, possibly finishing by
elimination (Gröbner/resultant, Plan B above) rather than a synthetic angle
chase.

via a continuation in α starting from a small, obviously-valid α, not a
blind global solve.
