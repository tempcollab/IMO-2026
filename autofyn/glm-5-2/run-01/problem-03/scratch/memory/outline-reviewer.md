# outline-reviewer per-role rules

ALWAYS: sanity-check the layer-cake identity `odd-index sum = ∫ ceil(N(t)/2) dt` and tower resistance with the CORRECT ≤n-mark budget (each mark splits one piece into two) — an over-budget split simulation produces false "violations" (imo-2026-03, round 1).

ALWAYS: when a self-similar lower-bound induction claims "top unsplit + rest resists by IH", check the parity: whole odd-index = top + rest-EVEN-index, while the IH lower-bounds rest-ODD-index — wrong direction (imo-2026-03, round 1).

NEVER: build two approaches that share the same load-bearing gap/wall in the same round (the single-gap trap) — hold the closer one in reserve (imo-2026-03: tower-induction & self-similar share both the lower-bound parity bug and the upper-bound case-(ii) wall).

ALWAYS: independently reproduce the load-bearing numerical claims (frontier recursion, parallel-halving-saturates-tower, dominant factorization identity) with exact Fraction arithmetic before APPROVE — the explorers' grid searches use floats/coarse grids and can mask tie artifacts (round 2: a non-strict parity check showed 1358 false "violations" from `.index()` finding tied L/2 pieces; the strict run gave 0).
NEVER: let two lower-bound slugs both bottom out on the same non-dyadic↔dyadic reduction step UNLESS their machinery is genuinely different language (continuous PL/variational vs discrete tree/frontier) AND each produces a different certified lemma — otherwise it is the single-gap trap (round 2: tail-count and tower-induction converge on the non-dyadic wall but via PL-continuity vs frontier-monotonicity; allowed because each yields a distinct importable lemma).
ALWAYS: verify a newcomer is ranked against the ESTABLISHED field (verified-milestone stale outcomes), not just against other cold-start newcomers, so its Elo anchors to real opponents (round 2: majorization-upper cold-start compared against tail-count/tower-induction/d-potential, not only its absence of outcomes).
NEVER: present verified-n=1..k numerics for a monotonicity/exchange claim as a proof — flag it as a CONJECTURE with the mechanism to prove and warn the builder against the B3 type-enumeration circularity trap (round 2: majorization-upper's "tower-is-hardest" exchange is conjecture verified n=1..4, must be a genuine smoothing not a type check).

ALWAYS: spot-check explorer numerics with `Fraction`-exact `python3` before approving — round 3 the lower-nondyadic explorer reported the V-shape value at q=2.5 as D=3 but the exact value is D=2 (minor slip, qualitative point held); a load-bearing number that is wrong undermines the outline.
NEVER: approve a charging/identity-based lower-bound slug without checking the identity's scope — round 3 gaps-leftover's `D = Σ(gaps)+leftover` holds ONLY for m=2n+1 (Xiang uses exactly n marks, odd piece count); the lower bound must hold for ALL ≤n-mark refinements including fewer marks / even m (no leftover). Flag the even-m / fewer-marks case as a scope gap the builder must close.
ALWAYS: when three slugs share one wall (round 3: tail-count/tower-induction/gaps-leftover all on G1-lower), approve them this round if each has a certifiable independent sub-result AND genuinely different framing — but flag the shared-wall-plateau risk so the orchestrator tells next round's outliner to put ≥1 approach on a framing far from the reduction, not a fourth lens on the same wall.
ALWAYS: when a slug's headline CONJECTURE is falsified mid-run (round 4: Max-bound `D*≤M/2^n` killed by `(7,6,5,3)/21`, ratio 8/7), drop its Elo below the steady-advance slugs even if it offers a sound replacement — the falsification is a real setback the ranking must reflect; the replacement's soundness keeps it above cold-start newcomers but not above slugs that never faltered (imo-2026-03, round 4).
ALWAYS: when an LP/Farkas-dual slug claims its wall is "weaker" or a "cheap kill," check strong duality — `min primal = max dual` makes "dual objective ≥ 1" EQUIVALENT to "primal min ≥ 1," not weaker. Flag the oversell; a dual framing's value is a different ATTACK ANGLE and different certified sub-results, not a difficulty reduction (imo-2026-03, round 4).
NEVER: approve a "V(3) provable from the certified n=2 base" claim without checking BOTH regimes — pairing `a_1` (non-dominant `a_1<2a_2`) puts two `a_2`'s at positions 1,2 and cancels, but in the DOMINANT case `a_1≥2a_2` pairing puts `a_1−a_2 ≥ a_2` at position 1 and the bound fails (witness `(8,3,2,2)/15` → margin 5, not ≤ M_2/4); dominant needs HALVING (the MB-Dom n=3 instance). The V(3) proof is a two-case split, not the single pairing move (imo-2026-03, round 4).

ALWAYS: when an upper-bound outline claims "prove a_{n+1} ≤ 1/D_n for tower-tail" as the hard step, check whether the tower-tail family includes a_1 < 2^n (non-dominant) — there S < D_n so a_{n+1} = 1/S > 1/D_n (the halving bound exceeds the target), but D* = 0 (pair cascade, verified exact). The "hard step" is either trivial (dominant: a_1 ≥ 2^n ⟹ S ≥ D_n) or FALSE-as-stated (non-dominant: D* = 0, not 1/S). The real hard step is proving D* = 0 for non-dominant configs via the pair cascade (a conjecture from numerics, not a proof). Flag the conflation of the halving upper bound with D* (imo-2026-03, round 5).

ALWAYS: verify a 5th-framing newcomer is genuinely far from the converged field by checking its IDENTITY mechanism, not just its name — xor-overlap's D = D_F + D_R − 2C is a bilinear parity correlation (integral of a product of two {0,1}-valued step functions), which is structurally different from PL geometry, spine cancellation, per-pair charging, and static LP certificates. A genuine 5th framing has a different OBJECT, not just a different name for the same object (imo-2026-03, round 5).

ALWAYS: when an outline proposes "exact pairing into equal pairs" as a strategy for a case (e.g. split-bottom + pair-rest), check whether exact pairing is even ACHIEVABLE for a concrete instance before approving — for imo-2026-03 compressed config (5,3,2)/10 (n=2), exact pairing is IMPOSSIBLE for ALL x in (0,1/D_n] and ALL split choices (structural obstruction: 3 distinct rest values, 9 pairing patterns all force x=0 or x>1/D_n). The bound D*≤1/D_n still holds (via near-equal distribution, D=1/1750), but NOT via exact pairing. A "pairing feasibility as PL function of x" is a category error — pairing is a discrete 0/1 indicator, not continuous, so IVT cannot apply. Kill the exact-pairing strategy; route to the packing/optimization framing instead (round 6).

NEVER: let an outline assert "the exact-pairing feasibility of {rest} is a PL function of x with finitely many breakpoints" — pairing feasibility is DISCRETE (a multiset either admits an equal-pair partition or not), not piecewise-linear. The PL structure is on D itself (as a function of all split points), not on pairing feasibility. Conflating the two produces a false IVT argument (imo-2026-03, round 6, O1 split-bottom).

ALWAYS: when a superincreasing argument claims "balance ⟹ block" (fragments at − = 0), check whether the superincreasing property constrains the ACTUAL objects in the balance equation — tower pieces are superincreasing (each 2^k > sum of smaller), but FRAGMENTS are arbitrary pieces of the top 2^n, NOT tower pieces, so the superincreasing property does NOT forbid a fragment-tower balance. The mechanism only works if the deficit is covered by tower pieces alone; fragments participating in the cover escape the constraint. Flag this as an insufficient mechanism needing strengthening (imo-2026-03, round 6, tail-count route A).

ALWAYS: when a greedy/monovariant upper-bound strategy claims "the remainder terminates ≤ a_{n+1}", check the remainder formula directly — for the "match next smaller" cascade r_{n−1} = a_1 − (a_2+...+a_n) = 2a_1 + a_{n+1} − 1, which is LARGE for large a_1 (the large-top regime), not ≤ a_{n+1}. Falsified by (12,4,3,2)/21: greedy gives D=1/7 > 1/15 while a different strategy (halve a_1+a_4) gives D=1/21. A greedy cascade that's the PRIMARY spine of an upper-bound ADVANCE must be spot-checked on a concrete large-top compressed instance before APPROVE — the "0 violations" numerics are usually the FULL recursive search, not the specific greedy (imo-2026-03, round 7, majorization-upper).

ALWAYS: a finite exhaustive PL-vertex enumeration IS a legitimate proof for fixed small n (it is casework over a finite combinatorial set, NOT the round-2-forbidden "grid numerics" which sample a continuum) — BUT only if the vertex set V_n is PROVEN complete (exhausts the PL-vertex set: every PL vertex is a tie-structure set-partition with sum=D_n, solved linearly). The distinction: grid numerics = continuum sample (forbidden as proof); vertex exhaustion = finite casework (legitimate if complete). A partial enumeration that misses mixed mark-distributions (e.g. "split top into 3 + split a tower") is verification, NOT proof (imo-2026-03, round 7, vertex-enum-n3).
