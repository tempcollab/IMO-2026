ALWAYS: identify cross-route shared lemmas and assign one builder to certify them early. periodic-set-iteration's Theorem 1 (iteration on a periodic set is a single cycle from the start) is the clean "lift=L / from-n=1" mechanism needed by 3 of 5 routes; the bounded-diff lemma `≤ rad(a_1)` is needed by all 5. ALWAYS: when an outline prescribes the SAME load-bearing refinement in two sibling slugs (e.g. "last-period covering bound, capacity ≤ω(m), demand T_*"), treat them as COUPLED — build ONE and hold the other out, because if the shared sub-move is refuted both die together (single-gap trap, round 2: small-prime-window-lemma / periodic-set-iteration / bounded-diff-finite-state all converged on one covering move).
NEVER: accept "F_n stabilizes ⇒ modulus L=∏∪F_∞" — the full small-prime support family over-counts the modulus (30030 vs true kernel product 30 for a_1=15); the stabilizing object is M'_n (minimal hitting sets), L=∏∪M'_∞ (round 2 correction).
ALWAYS: empirically test the "spacing fact alone suffices" claim before trusting a spacing-based B1' attack — at a_1=15, n=40, three large past primes touch only 2 of 15 window slots, so spacing alone is insufficient and the covering bound is the real heart (round 2).
NEVER: let a slug's "different mechanism" claim stand without checking whether its crude count goes the wrong way — v_p-counting's crude RHS ~ n·log n/R eventually exceeds LHS ~ n (wrong direction for contradiction); the refinement is where the real coupling hides (round 2).

ALWAYS: empirically re-verify the outliner's "X/X a_1 tested" empirical claims on the outline's OWN designated hard-regime cases before registering — round 3 cross-intersecting-anchor claimed "(B) cross-intersecting M'_∞ holds 12/12 incl 4-prime cases" but it is FALSE for a_1=135/105/385 (e.g. {2,5}∩{3,7}=∅ for a_1=135, the outline's own R-small real-test case). The check was only run on small-|M'_∞| cases where cross-intersection is trivially forced. A false empirical premise killed the whole distinctive crux; the approach was CUT (RETHINK, not registered) instead of APPROVED.
NEVER: accept a "distinctive crux (B)" that is actually spurious in the close — round 3 cross-intersecting-anchor step 3 invoked (B) cross-intersection with a BACKWARDS containment ("σ_i⊇h_i" — but h_i is a hitting set that INTERSECTS σ_i, not contained in it). When a "distinctive" crux is spurious in the close, the surviving route is the sibling's wall — single-gap trap.
