- Distinct openings: (1) **One-parameter angle construction / moving-configuration route.** Numerical solutions for fixed `ABC` form a continuous one-parameter family: choosing the common angle `x=∠KBA=∠ACL` appears to determine the remaining geometry (equivalently two further angles) and hence `K,L`. This suggests treating the hypotheses as a recursive ray-intersection construction rather than as six unrelated incidences, and looking for a quantity preserved while the parameter moves. The target quantity `OM²-ON²` was zero to about `10^-14` throughout many samples. The hard step is to find a synthetic invariant or a direct similarity between two parameter values; uniqueness/existence of the parameterized construction is secondary because the given configuration already exists. (2) **Homothety/rotation encoding of the three angle equalities.** Each equality canonically supplies an oriented similarity between a pair of rays: `(BK,BA)` with `(CA,CL)`, `(BL,BK)` with `(NL,NC)`, and `(CL,CK)` with `(MB,MK)`. This is a constructive “compose the three local similarities” framing; stop once the composition is identified. Its hard step is controlling orientations from the inside-angle assumptions. (3) **Circumcenter-locus reduction.** Since `A,K,L` determine `O`, `OM=ON` is exactly the assertion that `O` lies on the perpendicular bisector of `MN`; with `A=0,B=b,C=c`, this is the linear identity `2 O·(c-b)=(|c|²-|b|²)/2`. Because `2O·K=|K|²` and `2O·L=|L|²`, one only needs an experimentally discoverable linear decomposition of `c-b` in the `K,L` basis whose matching quadratic identity follows from the three angle relations. This is an analytic discovery route, not a brute-force endgame. (4) **Special-value interpolation.** In symmetric test triangles the conclusion is forced by reflection: for `C=(0,1)`, numerical solutions satisfy `L` equal to the reflection of `K` across `y=x`, and `O` lies on that axis; in the equilateral case the analogous median symmetry appears. If the moving-family induces a direct similarity on `(A,K,L,O)`, two easy parameter limits/symmetric values could pin down the entire locus, adapting the “two special configurations determine a line” crux from `aimo-1007`.
